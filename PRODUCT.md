## Users

The primary users are CTOs, founders, and engineering or data leaders responsible for a difficult or business-critical system. They arrive when failure is expensive, accumulated complexity is slowing the team down, or a consequential architecture and implementation decision needs senior hands-on ownership. Their job is to determine whether Sabino Software can understand the system, reduce the risk, and help deliver a useful result without introducing consultancy dependence.

Potential employers and recruiters evaluating Felipe Sabino for a long-term senior hands-on engineering role are a secondary audience. The site may support those conversations, but it must remain centered on Sabino Software and qualified technical work rather than becoming a generic resume or job-seeker profile.

Technical peers and collaborators may use the systems lab and journal to evaluate the thinking, code, and experiments behind the work.

## Product Purpose

Sabino Software is an engineer-led software consultancy founded by Felipe Sabino. It provides technical consulting and advisory work as well as direct implementation across data platforms, backend and distributed systems, applied AI, cloud and platform infrastructure, and hardware-integrated products.

The website makes the practice legible: what problems it can take on, how Felipe works, what has changed in real systems, and how to begin a high-context conversation. Consulting is a first-class outcome, not merely a prelude to implementation; an engagement may resolve an architecture decision, review a system, establish a recovery or modernization path, or continue through production delivery.

Success means generating qualified conversations with people who have a real system, constraint, and desired outcome. Project work, embedded or fractional technical leadership, longer consulting engagements, and suitable long-term employment conversations are all valid outcomes. High-context fit matters more than inquiry volume.

## Positioning

Sabino Software combines senior technical consulting with hands-on engineering in the same accountable practice. Felipe Sabino stays close to the work from investigation and architecture through production code, verification, documentation, and transfer of ownership. The offer is neither a generic development agency nor detached slide-only advisory.

The practice works across boundaries that are often split among vendors: data movement, backend services, cloud infrastructure, developer workflows, applied AI, and hardware or edge constraints are treated as parts of one operating system. AI agents and purpose-built bots provide leverage for research, analysis, implementation, automation, and verification, while technical judgment and responsibility remain human and explicit.

## Operating Context

- Engagements can begin with a focused technical question, architecture or implementation review, troubled platform, modernization program, embedded engineering need, or fractional technical-leadership responsibility.
- The durable delivery sequence is frame, design, build, and transfer: identify the real constraint and definition of done; choose an architecture that fits both system and team; implement the critical path; then leave code, context, automation, and operating capability with the client.
- Consulting and implementation may be purchased independently or together. Advice should be concrete enough to guide a decision, and implementation should preserve the reasoning behind it.
- AI agents and bots are part of the working environment. They increase research breadth, execution speed, and verification coverage; they do not replace review, deterministic checks, confidentiality boundaries, or Felipe's accountability.
- Work can be focused, embedded, fractional, part-time, full-time, or longer-term. The practice is based in Joinville, Brazil, works worldwide, and is comfortable with remote, asynchronous collaboration and intentional timezone overlap.
- Prospective clients evaluate fit through public production case studies, public-safe confidential extracts, open-source systems, workshops, journal entries, and direct contact with Felipe.
- The public experience record distinguishes Felipe's career history from Sabino Software client endorsement.

### Constraints

- The current site is a build-free static GitHub Pages project. Readable source lives in `assets/css/site.css` and `assets/js/site.js`; checked-in minified counterparts are what the pages load.
- Contact is intentionally handled without publishing a personal email address. The Cloudflare Worker in `contact-worker/` validates Turnstile, rate-limits submissions, stores accepted messages, and may send a private notification.
- Umami analytics may measure page views and categorized interactions, but tracking must not include form values, user identifiers, or URL query strings.
- Confidential client or product identities must remain omitted unless Felipe explicitly approves disclosure. Public-safe extracts may describe engineering surfaces, constraints, and technologies without enabling unsupported claims about the organization or deployment.
- Company logos represent Felipe's factual career history and never imply endorsement of Sabino Software.
- Evidence must remain precise about project maturity. Experiments, prototypes, research forks, and pre-alpha work must not be presented as production recommendations or finished products.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, client endorsements, benchmarks, revenue, pricing, outcomes, certifications, or usage metrics.
- The site may support recruiting conversations, but must not add an Open to Work treatment or reposition Sabino Software around active job seeking without explicit approval.

## Product Principles

1. **Stay close to the system.** Consulting, architecture, code, operations, and team context remain connected so decisions survive contact with production.
2. **Make decisions executable.** Advice should become a testable path, and important technical choices should be proven through the smallest useful implementation.
3. **Use leverage without outsourcing judgment.** AI agents and automation expand what one senior engineer can investigate, build, and verify; accountability, confidentiality, and deterministic validation remain explicit.
4. **Leave ownership with the team.** Good delivery creates understanding, local workflows, documentation, automation, and operational confidence rather than dependence on the consultant.
5. **Show proof without breaking trust.** Use real code, artifacts, public cases, and carefully bounded extracts; state maturity and confidentiality honestly.
