04 / The engineer behind the studio

Hands-on is not a phase of the career.

Felipe Guilherme Sabino founded Sabino Software to stay close to the difficult work: understanding an unclear system, shaping the architecture, writing the code, and helping a team own what comes next.

Across 19+ years in professional software engineering and roughly a decade in production data platforms, he has worked inside startups, scale-ups, consultancies, and large enterprises—including Nubank, Conta Azul, Transfeera, Accenture, NTT DATA, and TOTVS.

The through-line is systems thinking with practical range: distributed data, backend services, cloud platforms, developer experience, applied AI, and increasingly hardware-integrated software. Titles change. The commitment to build, explain, and improve the real thing does not.

  • PRINCIPLE_01

    Code is part of the strategy

    The important trade-offs become visible when the design is executable.

  • PRINCIPLE_02

    Make the system legible

    Documentation, observability, and local workflows are production features.

  • PRINCIPLE_03

    Optimize for ownership

    The best handoff leaves capability, not dependency on a consultant.

  • PRINCIPLE_04

    Question the inherited shape

    Modernization starts by understanding why the old system became hard.
