# Living systems — local implementation and verification

This batch starts from the deployed, verified `79ed0944dfe0d43b2091a17cca140a9358a3e352` on `feat/living-systems-expansion`. It is local only: no push, deployment, DNS/infrastructure change, production secret handling, signing-key replacement or production data mutation is authorized or performed. The initial working tree was clean. Changes in this batch were made in explicitly assigned files; no reset, stash or clean was used.

## Playable systems

- Physical pointer ownership spans Interact, conversation choices, Leave, movement, attacks, PTT and modal replacement. A gesture cannot activate a newly mounted button. Native keyboard activation remains separate. Rapid touch, cancellation, capture loss, drag and multitouch are covered. The exact reported phone fault did not reproduce in the first desktop Chromium emulation; the lifecycle hazards were nevertheless reproduced and fixed without an arbitrary cooldown timer.
- Walk lock, run lock, Stop and Go home use ordinary movement and bounded hierarchical navigation. Explicit input, menus, danger, death and obstacles stop travel. Doors require permission. Home routing reaches the actual owned entrance and reports unreachable routes. Persistent surface actors retain identity, position, destinations, wounds and memories across chunks, reduced simulation detail and restore.
- The field satchel is authority-owned. Deterministic enemy and wildlife rewards become visible exclusive-then-public ground drops; actual tool ownership, distance, inventory capacity, claim ownership, permanent receipts and room/floor identity govern gathering, hunting, claiming and crafting. Four wildlife families have distinct reactions and ecological materials. Ten recipes combine materials into tools, processed supplies and consumables.
- Three original local charter families provide contacts, discovery, joining, duties, dues, ranks, rivals, departure and reconciliation. Witnessed acts create subjective remembered traits and bounded local reports. Guards warn, fine, pursue and strike according to known reports. Door permissions, occupation and trespass feed consequences; individual reactions affect dialogue, aid, fear and prices. Craft rank one gives extended workshop access; eligible rank-two construction receives actual fee relief. Higher rank is recognition, not unimplemented leadership commands.
- Inspectable signs connect useful names, services, access, ownership and heraldic shapes with buildings, field panels and the explored map. Shape and text supplement color. Mobile panels use accessible tabs, short pages and actual command targets. They retain focus and close reliably during pending operations.
- Players buy/rent vacant existing property or parcels, store materials, grant guests, lock, furnish, rest, renew and construct five kinds of workstation. Placement previews the real footprint, requires an explicit second confirmation and checks access before charging. Named workers travel to collect inputs, work, carry outputs and deposit them once. Six production chains have wages, shifts, queues, shortages, loyalty and finite shop demand. Worker safety and observed conduct matter.
- An original three-floor underground complex connects exploration, signal puzzles, gated passages, shortcuts, secrets, hazards, safe rests, survivor rescue, material caches, minibosses and phased bosses. Melee follow-ups, ranged and magical projectiles, guard and dodge have authoritative timing. Rescued people become named surface workers, and recovered plans reduce eligible rebuilding costs. Floors have original animated presentation and material/audio identity. Paid rescue returns to a real surface anchor; it is explicitly separate from pathfinding.
- Original eight-bar adaptive themes replace random plucked-note sequencing. Five time identities, seven instrument tables, motifs, harmonic plans and cadence rules vary by actual place, population, civilization, danger, dungeon, home, reputation and production. Transitions respect phrase boundaries. Twelve material contacts vary with movement, weight, moisture and interiors. Species calls and semantic construction, machine, guard, crime, weapon and pickup families are bounded and spatial. Sparse social sound requires actual people and appropriate hours; it cannot follow the player through empty wilderness.

## Authority, compatibility and privacy

`livingSystems: 1` is additive to the existing protocol 3. Genuine older `/ws` clients retain their existing path. Current lives cannot downgrade the capability to bypass ownership/movement checks. New loot, civic, property, actor, fauna and dungeon ledgers occupy versioned **private operator snapshots**. The existing signed public checkpoint and Pear callback schema remain unchanged. No transient microphone content, sound/VFX event queues or projectiles are persisted. **The older public Pear replica alone cannot restore these new private ledgers.**

Actual character-creation cosmetics use a different appearance seed from the original resident. Body custody therefore resolves the resident's identity and authoritative location independently of cosmetics. Dead, removed, remote or already occupied residents are refused before allocating a new field life. Authenticated older unbound lives resume their stored identity and position using retained origin/expedition history; a changed cosmetic seed or incoming resume pose cannot claim another body. Regression tests cover real created lives, duplicate/dead admission, distant surface travel and underground reconnect/recall.

Modern shared combat uses a frozen, server-derived expedition kit. Historical personal items remain in the save; unverified imported items/upgrades cannot mint trusted loot. Full authoritative migration of all old equipment, levels and player health is still separate work. The inherited personal-health model must not be described as complete anti-cheat.

Modern surface rescue is an explicit coordinate-free authority command. Its admitted refuge, paid field-coin loss, cooldown and request sequence persist. A fresh valid current-life response supplies the destination; an old response cannot relocate a later life. Shared shrine transfer is truthfully unavailable until atomic body reservation/possession transfer is implemented. Solo and genuine legacy mind-travel behavior remains available. Surface recovery is an allowed paid rescue, not a claim that the server independently proved a legacy client's death.

`/voice` retains its distinct authenticated transport and limits. The only spatial integration adds authoritative floor isolation and stops a speaking burst during a floor/recovery transition. Protocol tests use synthetic binary frames, never recorded microphone input. No new production voice endpoint, credentials or environment defaults were introduced.

The initial attributed ground-render stall was 1,654.9 ms. Reused worker-generated grain atlases match the old output exactly across 33 samples (zero changed bytes in 1,048,576 checked), and avoid that repeated synchronous work. Final workstation Chromium portrait travel samples ran 14 seconds at 320×568 and 390×844: RAF p95 at most 16.8 ms, no intervals above 33.5 ms, game-work p95 4.1–4.5 ms. A separate successful-hit phase-two boss sample reduced HP from 198 to 170 over native held input; its eight-second capture had 480 frames, p95 16.8 ms, no intervals above 33.5 ms and no console errors. These are isolated workstation measurements, **not physical phone FPS guarantees** or a four-hour endurance test.

Cold room/world admission still measured about 103 ms in one server sample, including about 70.6 ms of generation. Warm coordinator work was roughly 0.5–2.7 ms. Native audio scheduling peaked at 4.2 ms in the final performance sample; generated-buffer work peaked at 3.6 ms. Saved raw tails are retained rather than presented as universal ceilings.

A dense accepted stress frame with 96 workers, 384 charter memberships, 64 estates, 512 queued stations, 64 drops with contributors and 160 pending rewards is 18,208 JSON nodes / 377,050 bytes. Only 16 pending rewards are summarized at once; all 160 remain in private state and the UI gives the total. The existing 20,000-node guard remains unchanged, and this fixture stays below the review’s conservative 512 KiB per-frame budget. The existing transport still has its separate character and buffered-socket guards. This is a protocol capacity test, not a normal per-frame load target.

## Concrete continuation, in priority order

1. Physical Android/iPhone/iPad listening and touch checks: speakers/headphones, Bluetooth routing changes, permission denial, calls/interruptions, background return, OS keyboard and installed PWA behavior. Browser emulation cannot certify these. Human listening must judge composition fatigue and material realism; waveform and scheduling tests cannot.
2. Atomic shared mind/body transfer and migration of historical equipment/progression/player health into authority. Add a separately reviewed signed replica extension for private-domain disaster recovery.
3. Extend autonomous actor travel through stair/interior portals. Persistent surface actors cross chunks and settlements; dungeon residents are currently floor-bound. Add road-junction sign publication and longer inter-settlement courier consequences.
4. Ground-up house shells/walls/roofs, property damage/upkeep, full worker needs and multi-estate carts/logistics. Current construction is real workstations and furnished existing homes/parcels, not a complete freeform architecture editor.
5. Guard arrest/confiscation, disguises/evidence investigations, higher guild leadership and civilization-scale politics. Current local witness/report/fine/pursuit consequences are working slices.
6. More fauna anatomies/ecologies, deeper dungeon encounter grammars and repeat-run remix rules. Meaningful cleared content currently persists; this batch does not promise an infinitely replenished handcrafted dungeon.
7. Stage cold server generation farther off the main event loop and profile full rooms on low-end hardware. Current bounded registers are explicit: 2,048 actors, 128 field lives, 64 estates/four per owner, 96 workers and finite permanent receipt registers. Saturation preserves existing history and refuses new admissions.
8. Actual weather and calendar special-night events, footwear equipment semantics and more recorded surface bases. Audio supports those signals without fabricating a weather simulation; current dirt/gravel/mud and metal designs layer shared licensed bases with original textures.
