# Destino: Stíchos — adaptation notes

Theo Bishop is the traveler's identity on Stíchos. Mind transmission placed him in a priest's body by mistake. He has been stranded for ten Earth years, equivalent to twenty Stíchoi years, without contact with his base. The present year is 3886. One local year lasts six Earth months. He remembers other planets and eras; Stíchos is special because the Sallas family's secret may be his way home.

Stíchos is extremely cold, with blue landscapes extending into the horizon. The inhabitants use the Rømer temperature scale. Cequin resembles rosemary and is necessary to breathe in the cold. Highly developed botany supplies food and medicine directly from plants. Industrial production threatens this society's established botanical practices.

Six great families are directed by priests, often their oldest members. The priests are revered as “originais,” true children of Prometheus. Their hidden Cúpula do Destino directs each family's destiny. Theo must publicly endorse decisions he does not support, and has avoided influencing history while trying to maintain his cover.

Priest Orlando of the Brown clan publicly seeks support for industrial food and medicine. Other clans perceive a threat. Theatre and surrealist paintings carry competing political messages before open war. Theo resolves to intervene cautiously to prevent catastrophe, despite his previous restraint. The Sallas secret is the key to that intervention and possibly to his return.

The priest's cathedral is Gothic with a futuristic character: colored stained glass, monumental architecture, and radio-era technology. The author's reference matrix combines world-war tension, industrial-revolution economics, clan society, radio communication, Gothic cathedrals, surrealism, Prometheus, dictatorship, Neoplatonism, botany, and an ice age.
