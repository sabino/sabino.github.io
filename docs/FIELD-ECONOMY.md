# Authoritative field economy — integration and balancing

This original Verso system is a server-side ledger, not a second client inventory. It supplies playable combat recovery, hunting, tool-based gathering, ten recipes, local trading and atomic costs/rewards for guilds, property and underground encounters. All names, tables and recipes were authored for this project. No external art, music, code or proprietary game content is used in this module.

## Ownership and authority

`src/stichos/field-loot.ts` exports `FieldEconomy`, `validFieldEconomySave`, `validFieldEconomyFrame`, `FIELD_ITEMS`, `FIELD_RECIPES`, `FIELD_RULES`, `WILDLIFE_HEALTH`, `rollFieldLoot` and `fieldSlots`. The field model never imports or trusts the historical personal inventory. The owning room supplies actual target identity, position, world space, role, biology and current context.

Create `new FieldEconomy(world.seed, savedSystems?.field)`. Persist `save()` only inside versioned private operator `systems`, or the single-player equivalent. An absent save is the additive migration. An invalid present save fails closed; never silently replace it with an empty economy. Nothing changes the deployed public signed checkpoint schema or signing identity.

Use a stable authenticated life/member identity consistently for `ensure(id)`, the field peer ID, contributors, transaction ownership, guilds and property. Do not use a reconnect's transient socket ID. The root integration must map `SharedCombatDeath.contributors` to stable identities and resolve all user intent against the member's actual server position. Room codes, client-supplied balances and imported legacy items are never evidence of authority.

`ensure` issues exactly one settled-life kit per identity: 24 field coins, four timber, four stone, three fiber and one knife. It does not refill on reconnect. Knife, hatchet, pick and spear cannot be sold at the general market. Deliberately starting a new independent life is not a migration of an old life or a claim to its property.

## Integration sequence

1. At negotiated `livingSystems: 1` membership, call `ensure(stableId)`. Send `frame({id,spaceId,x,y}, elapsedSeconds)` only to that authenticated member. It contains their satchel and at most 64 nearby drops in the same world space. `validFieldEconomyFrame` rejects malformed packets. Send domain updates on mutations and a modest location refresh cadence, not every render frame.
2. Before each authoritative combat operation, retain relevant enemies by ID: SharedCombat deletes defeated records from its snapshot. For each actual death, call `spawnDeath` once with that retained actor's identity, position, kind `enemy`, role, a bounded difficulty based on max HP/encounter tier, converted contributors and room time. It creates an immutable `death:<actorId>` receipt and `drop:<actorId>`. Do not call from client death reports. `canAdmitDeath` lets the authority check capacity before fatal damage; a full ledger must not silently award nothing or evict previous rewards.
3. Render `drop` position/name/rarity and a clear Recover/Dress interaction. Call `claim` with the current authoritative peer position. Radius is 2.2 world tiles, inclusive. All contributors can claim the shared bundle for 90 real seconds of room time; after that it is scavengable. There is one bundle and one successful claimant, not one reward per contributor. A full bag leaves both the bundle and balances untouched. The successful response contains the exact authoritative reward and `scavenged` flag for sound/UI.
4. For a hunt, resolve the target in the current persistent `LivingWorld` frame and a collision-aware strike ray. `hunt(peer, fauna, time, {clearLine:true,...})` requires explicit line clearance and an owned knife or spear. It enforces reach, health and per-life cooldown, persists wounds and contributors, and returns `reaction: flee|defend`, damage, health and killed. Route the reaction to the actor's actual goal/target. On `killed:true`, immediately call `LivingWorld.defeat(target.id)` in the same authority action; a focused test proves the actor and reward never regenerate after both ledgers restore. Animal health is 12/36/54/44 for bird/grazer/boar/wolf. Knife hits for 8 at 1.8 tiles; spear hits for 18 at 2.8 tiles. Recovery is 0.8 seconds, preserved across reconnects.
5. A carcass needs an owned knife to dress. Wildlife yields biological materials, never coins or forged objects. Wolves yield hide, bone and fangs; no routine predator meat reward. Pass protected-species status, actual faction, biome and time from authority. `protected-hunt` on the fatal strike and `protected-harvest` on recovery are separate action hooks: the civic layer decides witnesses, legality and proportional consequences; avoid automatically fining twice for one hunt. Mere scavenging is identified, not assumed criminal. Meat has no spoilage timer because the current world has no coherent spoilage/cold-storage system.
6. `gather(peer, actualResource, time)` requires a hatchet for timber/resin and a pick for stone/ore; fiber is hand gathered. Root resolves an actual harvestable prop and its output, checks local law, then marks it removed only after successful atomic reward. The resource ID gets a permanent depletion receipt. One stroke yields 2–4 seeded units, with a 1.2-second recovery. Failed/full-bag actions do not deplete the prop.
7. `craft(peer, recipeId, actualStation, eventId)` consumes field balances at a nearby workshop/hearth or field surface. `sell(peer,item,quantity,actualMarket,eventId)` requires a nearby open market; actual hours, permission and reputation price multiplier are resolved by civic integration. The module accepts multipliers only in 0.25–2.0 and quantities 1–99. General market tools have zero value.
8. Other server-owned domains use `transact(actorId,cost,reward,eventId)`. Both bundles require `{coins,items}` with finite positive integer item quantities and known item IDs. Costs, rewards and receipt IDs must be authored by the authority, never copied from the wire. All balance changes and the receipt commit synchronously or nothing changes. Duplicate event IDs return `ok:false`; do not apply world consequences again. Prefix IDs with the originating domain and persist its serial. External inventory buffers/worker jobs must commit only after this succeeds and be included in the same room snapshot.

## Content and progression

The ten recipes form a coherent chain: knife/hatchet/pick, saw boards, work metal, cure leather, cook trail meals, weave dressings, hunting spear and bone/crystal ward. Boards/ingots/leather support construction; cooked meals support workers/expeditions; dressings support recovery; knowledge and relics support guild/dungeon progression. The spear is a real hunting upgrade and deliberately needs boards, bone and resin, connecting construction craft to biological gathering. A ward is an authoritative equipment token; gameplay effects must be explicitly consumed by the owning combat/expedition integration, not assumed from the item label.

Enemy role changes table identity: martial enemies carry fittings, scouts carry fiber/feathers, scholarly enemies carry knowledge/resin and wardens carry crystal/fittings. Difficulty increases useful quantity and currency, with uncommon/rare/exceptional bands. Addressed seeds include stable actor identity, species, role, biome and day/night context; changing hit order, contributor order, camera or reconnect never rerolls an existing receipt. Protected status does not increase yield. Distribution tests assess ranges and statistical variety across 5,000 enemies and 1,200 animal samples, rather than frozen random snapshots.

## Persistence, capacity and performance

Limits are explicit and tunable: 128 life satchels, 256 active drops, 2,048 wounded fauna, 16,384 combined receipt/reserved-claim slots, default 24 inventory slots with stack size 99 and maximum 64 slots. A live drop reserves its future claim receipt. Transactions cannot exhaust space needed by an outstanding claim. Receipts are never evicted to admit new loot; a capacity warning is a real admission stop. A longer-lived deployment can add a versioned compact historical receipt index, but cannot discard old anti-duplication facts.

Drops expire after 1,800 seconds of authoritative room time, including elapsed time while nobody is connected. Expiry removes the visible record only; the death receipt remains. The save rejects duplicate identities, unknown fields/materials, malformed addresses, invalid counts, impossible receipt relationships and audio/transient keys. It contains no microphones, audio, VFX or scheduled presentation work.

Node v26.8.1 local stress measurements (not a physical mobile guarantee) at 256 active drops: 500 frame samples, median 0.396 ms, p95 0.598 ms, maximum 0.873 ms. At 16,128 receipts plus 256 reserved claims, snapshot p95 improved from 22.718 ms to 3.178 ms by replacing generic cloning of flat immutable receipts with bounded shallow copies. Restore p95 improved from 45.686 to 20.638 ms. Maximum fixture JSON is 1,436,435 bytes; send public frames, never this ledger over every network tick. Restore is a room-load operation. Raw 500/30/30-sample traces are `.dream-loop/living-systems/field-loot/performance-before.json` and `performance.json`.

Focused gate: `node --experimental-strip-types --test tests/stichos-field-loot.test.ts`. Typecheck: `npx tsc --noEmit`. Root owns browser evidence, complete suite, multiplayer authority wiring, save integration and independent review; these are not claimed by unit tests alone.
