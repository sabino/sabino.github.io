# Local civic consequences and readable places

This batch adds original deterministic models in `src/stichos/civic-world.ts` and `src/stichos/world-signs.ts`. The models require the central authority, navigation, dialogue, renderer and field-economy adapters described below; they do not claim browser integration merely by exporting interfaces. The lead integrator owns those adapters and their end-to-end checks.

## State and actions

`new CivicWorld(seed, civilization?, save?)` admits actual generated `Settlement` objects through `factionsFor(settlement)`. Every known town has three original guilds: a watch, a craft or trade group, and a care or hunting group. Actual civilization industry, ecology, scarcity and collective organization shape their choices, laws, fines and values. IDs include seed, settlement ID and purpose; display names and heraldry do not become identity keys. The five rank titles and geometric emblems are original Verso content.

The playable authority loop is introduction → join and contribute field supplies → daily delivery or locally witnessed service → dues and ranks → benefits. Senior rival oaths prevent acquiring another senior charter. Refusal, departure, expulsion, restitution and re-entry are real state transitions. Twelve successful daily duties can reach the highest recognized charter rank; the final rank depends on contribution values. Rank one grants craft-charter workshop hours, and rank two grants eligible construction fee relief. Ranks three and four retain those benefits and record senior standing; worker sponsorship and civic leadership decisions remain explicit continuation work, not unlocked actions in this slice. Duties rotate deterministically by the existing 24-minute shared day. Costs consume trusted field inventory; showing a client inventory is insufficient. A paid charge does not erase anyone's memory.

- `discover(actorId, factionId)` must follow an actual introduction or close-reading a public charter.
- `setGuildContact(factionId, npcId)` binds up to four actual generated/persistent residents to a guild. Root should use the NPC who introduces the charter, matching their trade/role. This is an authority-only operation, never an actor ID accepted from the wire.
- `join`, `payDues`, `completeDuty`, `reconcile` receive a synchronous `CivicDebit` callback. Bind it to `field.transact(authenticatedActorId, cost, { coins: 0, items: {} }, receipt).ok`. Never construct cost/reward from client input. Failed transactions leave civic membership unchanged.
- `duty` returns actual required materials or a service action, description, due day and next benefit. `completeDuty` validates service evidence already held by guild contacts, after joining and during the current day. One completion per shared day; receipts prevent duplicate debit.
- `membership`, `memberships` and `faction` return copies. Root must apply the listed access/discount/worker benefits at their actual service transaction. A descriptive benefit is not a discount by itself.

`recordAction(action, witnesses, sight)` accepts **server-derived** outcomes and stable source receipts only. It assigns its own event ID. The world adapter resolves settlement, position, actor, victim, actual target, legality and nearby residents. Each event has a multidimensional interpretation: helpful, merciful, trustworthy, generous, cruel, violent, thief, trespasser, protector, hunter, entrepreneur, loyal, oathbreaker. Civilization priorities, guild purpose and persistent observer identity influence interpretation. Use `reputation(npcId, actorId)` for individual dialogue/prices/fear, and `factionReputation` for guild reactions. `townReputation` is a summary of known local accounts, **not** evidence available to every resident or guard.

Visual observation needs the same space, 12-tile proximity and the supplied line-of-sight function. A nearby sound from a serious offense reaches eight tiles but does not identify the actor. A report requires two living nearby participants, line of sight and six-tile range. Each hop preserves the named source and reduces confidence to 0.8; only two hops are allowed. Reports can reach another settlement only through actual moving people. They do not broadcast civilization-wide. Root should schedule a small number of actual conversations, rather than call all sender/receiver combinations each frame.

Guild penalties happen only when a bound contact sees or receives credible evidence. One isolated witness cannot instantly expel a member. Each applied offense is remembered once per membership. Aid and restitution can make reconciliation possible without deleting the original betrayal.

## Guard and door adapters

`updateGuard(actualGuard, visibleSuspects, worldSeconds, sight)` returns ordinary navigation destinations and states. A heard noise causes investigation; identified evidence produces a last-known position, warning/fine for minor charges, pursuit/arrest demand for serious charges, search after sight is lost, and a return/cooldown while an unresolved charge remains remembered. The function does not teleport, move an NPC, deal hidden damage or automatically imprison a player. Root must execute its destination through the shared actor navigation system, show the demand, and handle surrender safely. `settleFine` atomically pays restitution and resolves the same charge for other guards without erasing reputation. New witnesses may retain the memory but cannot demand payment twice.

`doorAccess` consumes the actual door `Prop`, corresponding `Tile`, shared hour and server-resolved property permissions. Owner, invited guest or matching key takes precedence; private doors, locked entrances and closed public hours are explicit. Inn access is all-day; shops/workshops, greenhouse, temple and civic hall have distinct hours. Denial returns the potential offense, not a completed crime. Root records trespass or burglary only after an actual unauthorized crossing or forced entry. Both direct Interact and auto-navigation must consult this decision. Historical player-owned homes must pass the legitimate owner adapter so the new law does not lock a player out of their existing home.

## Signs and original visual grammar

`signForProp(prop, tile, settlement, context)` uses generated doors/building kinds, noticeboards and banners. No parallel fabricated building schema exists. It returns the full generated place name, compact world label, owner or community, opening/permission state, actual services, optional transaction price, local laws/notices and guild charter. Optional prices must come from the real property/service adapter. Pass only services that the corresponding game interaction supports; current semantic defaults describe existing building roles.

`roadSign(origin, currentSettlement, surveyedSettlements)` names actual surveyed places with direction and distance, explicitly identifying distances as straight-line bearings. It does not pretend a road route was measured. `inspectWorldSign` validates same-space reading within 2.8 tiles. Full names remain readable while compact world labels truncate Unicode safely. Text is localization-ready through stable line keys and parameters; central DOM rendering must use text nodes or the existing escape helper.

`drawSignSymbol` draws twelve original silhouettes with code-native geometry: home, lodging, temple, workshop, growing house, store, civic hall, guard, guild, road, danger and dungeon. Shape and accessible labels carry meaning independently of color. Use matching `mapSymbol` in the minimap. Root should draw world icons at least 16 CSS pixels and panel icons at least 24; provide a touch-friendly Inspect action, visible focus, full text and a close button. Canvas geometry alone cannot satisfy screen-reader access.

## Persistence, capacity and safety

Save `CivicWorld.save()` only under versioned operator `privateState.systems`; do not change the deployed signed public checkpoint schema. Restore through `validCivicSave` and `CivicWorld` with the original world seed/profile. The snapshot contains deterministic towns, contacts, membership, actions, witness provenance and guard state. It contains no microphone content, audio events, VFX, runtime pointers or wall-clock timers. Return only necessary bounded personal/local views through negotiated `livingSystems: 1`, not the whole private evidence ledger.

Caps are explicit: 128 consequential towns, 1,024 memberships, 2,048 events, 8,192 knowledge records and 512 guards. Admission rejects at capacity rather than evicting existing identities or consequences. Witness admission is at most 24 per action, report hops at most two, guard target checks at most 128 supplied actors. The authority must also rate-limit commands and invoke guards at a bounded coarse cadence. A larger universe will need compact archival/migration support; this batch does not pretend a fixed in-memory journal is infinite.

Strict validation rejects unknown fields, dangling evidence, duplicate identities/source receipts, invalid coordinates, impossible confidence and unsafe object payloads. Gameplay commands still require existing authenticated-room authorization, authoritative target lookup and proximity; this model is not a wire authentication layer.

## Validation and continuation

Dedicated model tests cover actual generated town/building compatibility, faction determinism, atomic debit, service proof and rank progression, rival oaths, reconciliation, witness geometry, report provenance, audible anonymity, guard search/arrest demand, restitution deduplication, memory preservation, capacity, strict snapshot validation, owner/key/hour rules, generated sign text and distinct finite glyphs. Root supplies portrait screenshots and live model-to-world evidence after central integration.

Concrete next depth increments: criminal/scholarly/faith branches beyond the first three local charters; individually authored techniques for every rank; prison/confiscation and guarded transport; disguise evidence; allied-settlement treaties with actual couriers; property crime investigation using physical evidence; richer personal relationship interpretation. These are not claimed as implemented by this module. Workers, property production and dungeon rescue should call the existing service/build/rescue/protect actions and use the same contact/report ledger.

## Provenance

All names, emblems, text and code in these two modules are original. Design principles were researched from public primary materials linked in [the batch reference notes](LIVING-SYSTEMS-REFERENCES.md): Xbox Wire's Fable living-population explanation, Bethesda's Fallout reputation and Skyrim factions articles, and Wube's worker scheduling discussion. No proprietary dialogue, art, layouts, uniforms, audio, names, implementation or exact effects were copied.
