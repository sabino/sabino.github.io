# BRAVOCALYPSE! 3D asset credits

- **Business Man**, Quaternius, CC0. Animated male performer. Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html . GLB mirror: https://www.get3dmodels.com/characters/business-man/ .
- **Suit**, Quaternius, from Ultimate Modular Women. Animated female performer. Upstream CC0 source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularwomen.html . Model page: https://poly.pizza/m/sOUciDsoVV . Mirror: https://www.get3dmodels.com/characters/suit/ . Also attributed under the model page's CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Both characters retain the artist's complete original skinned face, hair, eyes, body, walk, run, idle and other skeletal animations. BRAVOCALYPSE! adds fitted seating IK, receiving/playing poses, material variations and physics. Upstream men/women licenses checked 2026-09-11.
- **Violin** and **Flute**, Poly by Google, CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). GLB mirrors: https://www.get3dmodels.com/toys-and-games/simple-violin-instrument/ and https://www.get3dmodels.com/tools-and-gadgets/simple-flute-model/ . BRAVOCALYPSE! adapts scale and placement.
- **Elegant Concert Harp**, Get 3D Models, CC Attribution. https://www.get3dmodels.com/art-and-sculptures/elegant-concert-harp/ . BRAVOCALYPSE! adapts scale and placement.
- **Food Kit 2.0**, Kenney, CC0. https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit . 52 selected GLB props. Original license accompanies the files in props/LICENSE.txt.
- **Grand piano, piano bench and concert chair**, original BRAVOCALYPSE Blender models. Reproducible source: `scripts/create-stage-assets.py` and `scripts/create-concert-chair.py`. These models replace the earlier procedural piano/chairs. No third-party piano model is bundled.
- **Cello and double bass**, original BRAVOCALYPSE Blender models with carved bodies, fingerboards, scrolls, tuning pegs, strings, bridges and endpins. Source: `scripts/create-string-instruments.py`; editable file: `assets/bravocalypse-strings.blend`. These replace the earlier resized violin variants.
- **Trumpet** and **Saxophone**, jeremy, CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Sources: https://poly.pizza/m/0Mj5XgeGtKJ and https://poly.pizza/m/6A2UAKdCNy7 . **Drum Set**, Zsky, CC BY 3.0. Source: https://poly.pizza/m/qWU9Q4flfQ . Files are in `ensemble/`; the source manifest records hashes. BRAVOCALYPSE! changes metre scale, playing placement and material finish. Runtime fractures preserve the artists' model surfaces. Model page attribution checked 2026-09-11.
- **Legacy maestro and artist heads**, original BRAVOCALYPSE Blender experiments, retained in the asset archive but no longer used by the game. Reproducible source: `scripts/create-cast-heads.py`.
- Flowers, bouquet, sunflower, teddy bear, rubber duck, balls, cushion, gifts, music stands, theater and the remaining instrument families: authored in the BRAVOCALYPSE! source using Three.js geometry.

Downloaded models are bundled locally; playback does not contact their source websites. No Online Sequencer code, sounds or models are redistributed.
